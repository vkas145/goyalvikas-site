When two lines cross, they make angles — and those angles follow secret rules! Let's uncover them.

✖️Intersecting lines: Lines that cross at one point, forming 4 angles.
📏Linear pair: Two angles next to each other on a straight line — they always add up to 180°.
🦋Vertically opposite angles: The angles across from each other when two lines cross — they're always EQUAL.
⊥Perpendicular lines: Lines that cross to make all 4 angles equal — each one is a right angle (90°).
🚃Parallel lines: Lines that never meet, no matter how far you extend them — like railway tracks.
🚧Transversal: A line that crosses two other lines, forming 8 angles. If corresponding angles are equal, the two lines are parallel!
