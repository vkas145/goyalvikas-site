How do we figure things out from numbers, like "who batted better" or "where are onions cheaper"? That's statistical thinking!

❓Statistical question: A question you answer by COLLECTING DATA, like "How tall are Grade 7 students?"
💬Statistical statement: A claim or summary based on numbers, like "Prices rose by ₹5 this month."
⚖️Average (Mean): Add up all the values, then divide by how many values there are.
🍬Fair-share idea: The average is what everyone would get if the total was shared out EQUALLY.
📈Min, Max & Range: The range = biggest value − smallest value. It shows how spread out the data is.
