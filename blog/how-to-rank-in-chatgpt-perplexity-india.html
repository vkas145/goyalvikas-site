How B2B Brands in India Can Get Cited in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  7 min read

When a B2B buyer in India asks Perplexity "best call quality monitoring tools for a mid-size BPO", the answer names two or three vendors with a one-line description of each. Being one of those names is now a distinct, achievable goal, separate from ranking on Google's results page — and separate from having a large marketing budget. Citation in these answers is driven more by content structure and credibility signalling than by domain authority alone, which is good news for smaller, specialist Indian B2B companies competing against larger, better-funded rivals.

Step 1: Find Out Where You Stand Today

Before changing anything, run your 15 to 20 highest-intent buyer questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview manually. Record three things for each: whether you're cited, how you're described, and which competitors appear instead. This baseline is what every later measurement compares against, and most Indian B2B teams discover their actual citation status is worse than they assumed — because it has simply never been checked.

Step 2: Fix the Content That's Closest to Being Cited

Pages that already rank on page one of Google for a query but aren't cited in the AI answer for the same query are your fastest wins. They already carry search-engine trust; they usually just aren't structured for extraction. Add a direct two-to-three sentence answer at the top of the relevant section, define any technical terms explicitly, and include a specific number or data point the model can quote with confidence.

Step 3: Strengthen the Signals Models Actually Weight

  • Named authorship with a real, checkable professional history — anonymous or "Team" bylines are a measurable disadvantage.
  • Specific, sourced numbers instead of vague claims ("reduced average handle time by 22% across 40 agents" beats "significantly improved efficiency").
  • Consistent entity information across your site, LinkedIn, and any directories — models cross-reference these to judge reliability.
  • Recency signals such as visible last-updated dates, since generative engines favour current information for anything involving pricing, regulation, or product comparisons.

Step 4: Give the Models Something Unique to Cite

Generic restatements of widely available information rarely get cited over a more authoritative competitor, because the model has no reason to prefer your version. Original data (even a small internal survey of your own client base), a named framework, or a specific case study number gives a model unique content it cannot get from any other source, which measurably increases citation odds for Indian B2B brands that are otherwise up against much larger competitors.

Step 5: Re-run the Baseline Monthly

Citation status shifts as models retrain and re-crawl, sometimes month to month, in a way traditional Google rankings rarely do. Treat the manual audit from Step 1 as a recurring exercise, not a one-time project, and track directional movement rather than expecting a stable, permanent citation once achieved.

What this is not: there is no way to pay for placement inside a ChatGPT or Perplexity answer today, and no legitimate technique guarantees a citation. This is a probability-improving process built on the same content-quality fundamentals that have always driven organic search, applied to a new surface.
