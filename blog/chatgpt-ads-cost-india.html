ChatGPT Ads Cost in India: Budgets, Bidding, and What to Expect

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  5 min read

The one hard number available at launch is the daily budget floor: self-serve campaigns in the ChatGPT Ads Manager can start with a budget as low as ₹725 per day. Everything above that floor — actual cost-per-click, competitive density by category, and how pricing evolves once all of the 50-plus launch-week brands are running campaigns simultaneously — is genuinely unknown at this early stage, and any specific benchmark being quoted this soon after launch deserves scepticism.

Why the Floor Number Matters More Than It Looks

A daily minimum of ₹725 is deliberately accessible, roughly comparable to a modest Google Ads test budget for a small or mid-size Indian business. This is a meaningful signal about what OpenAI is optimising for at launch: broad advertiser adoption and real bidding data across many categories, not maximising revenue per advertiser in the first weeks.

What Will Likely Drive Cost Once Data Exists

Based on how comparable auction-based ad platforms have evolved (Google Search, Meta, LinkedIn), expect cost to be shaped primarily by three factors once enough advertisers are active: category competitiveness (how many advertisers are bidding into similar conversation contexts), ad relevance and quality signals (how well your creative and landing page match the conversations you're targeting), and overall platform inventory relative to advertiser demand, which will shift as OpenAI decides whether and how to expand beyond Free and Go tier placements.

How to Budget a First Test Responsibly

  • Start at or near the ₹725 floor for the first one to two weeks purely to gather your own category-specific data.
  • Set a hard cap on total test spend — a fixed rupee amount you're comfortable losing entirely if the channel doesn't work for your category — rather than an open-ended daily budget you might forget to review.
  • Compare cost per qualified lead, not cost per click, against your existing channels once you have two to three weeks of data, since a cheap click on an unfamiliar inventory type is not directly comparable to a cheap click on a channel you already understand.

A Note on Timing Your Test

Being an early advertiser in a new auction-based ad system has historically meant lower competition and lower costs before other advertisers in your category catch on — this was true in the early days of Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and LinkedIn Ads. Whether that pattern repeats for ChatGPT Ads in India is not guaranteed, but it is a reasonable argument for running a small, low-risk test sooner rather than waiting for the category to mature and prices to potentially rise with competition.

What to ignore: any confident claim about "average ChatGPT Ads CPC in India" published in the first few weeks after launch. There is not yet enough advertiser volume or public data for such a number to be meaningful, and early figures circulating from a handful of launch advertisers are not a reliable benchmark for a different category or audience.
