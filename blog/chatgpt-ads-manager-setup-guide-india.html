How to Set Up a ChatGPT Ads Manager Campaign in India: Step-by-Step

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  6 min read

Self-serve access to the ChatGPT Ads Manager opens in India on September 4, 2026, letting businesses of any size sign up directly and bid for ad impressions with daily budgets starting at ₹725. For a marketer setting up a first campaign, the process shares some DNA with search advertising but has enough differences to warrant a deliberate first attempt rather than copying a Google Ads playbook directly.

Before You Open the Ads Manager

Do this preparation first, because it will save a rebuild later:

  • Write down the exact questions a buyer would ask ChatGPT that your product plausibly answers well — since targeting is context-based, this list functions similarly to a keyword list in search advertising, even though you won't be bidding on exact keywords the same way.
  • Prepare a landing page built for this traffic specifically, not your generic homepage, since the ad-relevance system considers the landing page content when deciding when to serve your ad.
  • Draft ad copy that reads like a direct, useful answer rather than conventional ad copy — a headline and description that would make sense appearing directly beneath a genuinely helpful AI response, not one that feels bolted on.

Setting Up the Campaign

1. Define a Narrow Test Scope

Pick one product or service line, one clear conversion action, and one geography or segment for your first campaign, resisting the temptation to test everything at once. A narrow first test produces a cleaner read on whether the channel works for your category at all.

2. Set the Budget Conservatively

Start near the ₹725 daily floor rather than your typical campaign budget. This is a new inventory type with unknown competitive dynamics; the goal of the first two to three weeks is learning, not scale.

3. Build Creative Around the Sponsored-Answer Format

Since the ad renders as a labelled, sponsored box beneath a complete AI answer with a headline, description, and call to action, write copy that complements rather than competes with the answer above it. Overly salesy copy is likely to feel jarring in this placement and underperform copy that reads as a natural, credible next step.

4. Set Up Conversion Tracking Before Launch, Not After

Because advertiser reporting is aggregated (impressions and clicks, not individual conversation data), your own landing page and CRM tracking is the only place you'll see what happens after the click. Confirm UTM parameters and lead-source tagging are working before spending a single rupee.

What to Watch in the First Two Weeks

Click-through rate relative to your other paid channels, cost per click at this new inventory type, and — most importantly — lead quality once those clicks reach your landing page. A channel that produces cheap clicks but low-intent leads is not a win just because the CPC looks attractive next to LinkedIn or Google.

A mistake to avoid: don't reuse your Google Search ad copy verbatim. Search ad copy is written to win a comparison against other listed results on a page; ChatGPT ad copy needs to work as a credible next step after a conversational answer, which is a different writing job even when the underlying offer is identical.
