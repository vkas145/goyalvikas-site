Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) Services for B2B Brands in India

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  6 min read

Answer Engine Optimisation is the practice of structuring content so it can serve as a direct, self-contained answer to a specific question. It predates the current AI wave — Google's featured snippets have rewarded this structure since roughly 2014 — but AEO has become a distinct service line in India because the same structural discipline that wins a featured snippet also improves the odds of being the source an AI model pulls from when it generates an answer.

What AEO Services Cover

An AEO engagement for a B2B brand in India typically includes four workstreams:

1. Question Inventory

Building an exhaustive list of the specific questions a buyer asks at each stage: definitional ("what is X"), comparative ("X vs Y"), and evaluative ("is X worth it for a company our size"). This inventory is built from sales call transcripts, support tickets, and search console query data, not guesswork.

2. Direct-Answer Content Blocks

Rewriting the opening of each relevant section so the answer appears in the first two to three sentences, in plain declarative language, before any elaboration, caveats, or narrative context. This single change is usually the highest-leverage AEO edit available on an existing content library.

3. Structured Data Markup

FAQPage, HowTo, and Article schema that makes the question-answer pairs explicit to crawlers, reducing the ambiguity a system has to resolve before it can extract and reuse the answer confidently.

4. Format-Specific Optimisation

Numbered steps for process questions, comparison tables for "X vs Y" questions, and defined-term callouts for definitional questions — matching the answer format to the question type rather than using one generic paragraph style everywhere.

Why AEO Matters More for B2B Than It Looks

B2B buyers ask narrower, higher-stakes questions than consumer searchers, and there are far fewer well-optimised answers competing for those questions in most Indian B2B categories. This means the effort-to-reward ratio for AEO work is often better in B2B than in the crowded consumer space, because a well-structured answer to "what should a mid-size logistics company budget for a WMS implementation in India" has almost no serious competition compared to a consumer query like "best budget smartphone".

A common mistake: treating AEO as an FAQ page tacked onto the bottom of existing content. The FAQ block helps, but the larger and more durable gain comes from restructuring the body content itself so every section leads with its answer, because that is the content AI systems and featured snippets actually lift from.

How AEO Fits Alongside SEO and GEO

AEO is best understood as the content-structure layer that sits underneath both SEO (rankings) and GEO (AI citations). A page built with strong AEO discipline tends to perform well on both surfaces simultaneously, which is why most Indian consultants now sell it as a foundational content standard rather than a standalone service.
