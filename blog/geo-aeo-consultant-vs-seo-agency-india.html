GEO/AEO Consultant vs SEO Agency in India: Which Does a B2B Company Need?

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  6 min read

Indian B2B marketing leaders evaluating this decision usually frame it as a budget question first, when it is actually a structural question about what kind of gap needs closing. A large retained SEO agency and an independent GEO/AEO consultant solve different problems, and the right choice depends on where a company already stands, not on which option sounds more current.

What a Traditional SEO Agency Is Built For

Agencies excel at sustained content production, technical SEO maintenance across a large site, and link-building programs that require ongoing outreach capacity. If your core problem is "we don't publish enough consistent, well-optimised content" or "our site has accumulated years of technical debt," an agency's team-based model and process discipline are usually the better fit, and most established Indian B2B SEO agencies have added GEO and AEO as line items to their existing retainers over the past year.

What an Independent GEO/AEO Consultant Is Built For

A specialist consultant is typically the better fit for a narrower, higher-judgment problem: auditing why a company that already ranks well isn't getting cited in AI answers, restructuring a small set of high-value cornerstone pages, or advising a leadership team on how to think about the category before committing budget to a larger program. Consultants trade production capacity for depth and speed of judgment on a specific question.

A Simple Decision Framework

  • Choose an agency if you need ongoing content volume, technical SEO maintenance, and a team that scales with a growing content library.
  • Choose an independent consultant if you need a focused audit, a strategy reset, or hands-on work on a small number of high-value pages, and you already have in-house capacity to execute recommendations.
  • Use both if you have an agency handling volume and want an independent GEO/AEO specialist to periodically audit and course-correct the agency's output against AI citation performance specifically, since agencies with broad mandates don't always prioritise this narrower measurement discipline on their own.

Questions to Ask Either Option

Regardless of which model you choose, ask for a specific example of a citation or ranking improvement they can walk through in detail, ask how they measure GEO and AEO performance separately from traditional SEO metrics, and ask what percentage of their current client work actually involves AI-citation-specific tactics versus a rebranded SEO offering. Vague answers to any of these three questions are a reliable warning sign in the Indian market right now, where the terminology has moved faster than the actual expertise in many agencies.

Cost expectation: independent GEO/AEO consulting engagements in the Indian B2B market are typically structured as a fixed-scope audit plus a lighter-touch monthly retainer for tracking, which tends to be a smaller commitment than a full agency retainer — useful for companies that want to test the category's ROI before committing to a larger program.
