GEO & AEO Strategy

SEO, GEO & AEO Consultant for B2B Marketing Consultants in India

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  8 min read

Every B2B marketing consultant in India built their SEO practice around one target: Google's ten blue links. That target has quietly split into three. A buyer researching "best call quality monitoring software for BPOs" today might see a Google result, a Google AI Overview summarising three vendors, and later ask ChatGPT or Perplexity the same question directly and get a spoken-word answer with zero links at all. Consultants who only optimise for the first of these three surfaces are optimising for a shrinking share of the buyer's actual research journey.

What SEO, GEO, and AEO Actually Mean for a B2B Practice

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is the discipline B2B consultants already know: ranking pages in traditional organic search results through keyword targeting, technical health, backlinks, and content depth. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is the newer discipline of getting a brand's content used, paraphrased, or cited inside AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) sits between the two: structuring content so it directly and concisely answers a specific question, which improves both featured-snippet placement in Google and the odds of being the source an AI model pulls from.

These three are not separate departments. A page built with a clear, quotable answer near the top, backed by named-author expertise and specific data, tends to perform across all three surfaces at once. The consultants building durable practices in India right now are the ones who stopped treating GEO and AEO as bolt-on services and started treating them as the natural evolution of doing SEO properly.

Why This Matters More for B2B Than for D2C in India

B2B buying in India is research-heavy and consensus-driven — a purchase decision usually involves 3 to 6 stakeholders who each do independent research before a group conversation happens. AI chat tools have become a fast, low-friction way for a procurement head, a CFO, or a category owner to get a quick synthesis before they ever talk to a vendor or a consultant. If your client's brand does not appear in that synthesis, the client is invisible at the exact moment a shortlist is being formed — often weeks before a Google search or a website visit happens at all.

This is a bigger risk for B2B than for D2C because B2B sales cycles are long and shortlists are small. Being excluded from three AI-generated answers during a six-month evaluation window can mean being excluded from the deal entirely, with no analytics event ever showing the loss.

What a Combined SEO, GEO & AEO Program Looks Like

1. Map the Questions, Not Just the Keywords

Traditional keyword research starts with search volume. A GEO- and AEO-aware version starts with the exact questions a buyer would type into ChatGPT during evaluation: "what should a mid-size NBFC look for in a call quality audit tool," "is X better than Y for Indian BPO teams," "how much does call QA software typically cost in India." These conversational, comparison-heavy, and cost-related questions are exactly what generative engines are asked most often, and they rarely show up in a traditional volume-sorted keyword list.

2. Answer First, Elaborate Second

AI models and featured snippets both favour content that states the direct answer in the first 2 to 3 sentences of a section, then elaborates with detail, data, or nuance afterward. This is the opposite of the narrative-building style much B2B content still uses, where the point is built up to gradually. Restructuring existing cornerstone content to lead with the answer is usually the single highest-leverage AEO change a consultant can make in an existing client's content library.

3. Build Named, Verifiable Expertise Into Every Page

Generative engines are trained to weight credibility signals heavily when choosing what to cite, because citing an unreliable source is a visible failure mode for them. Content attributed to a named expert with a public profile, specific years of experience, and real numbers (client counts, percentage improvements, scale figures) is consistently favoured over generic, unattributed corporate copy. For B2B marketing consultants in India, this means the personal-brand SEO work they may have treated as a nice-to-have for a founder is now a direct GEO input.

4. Use Structured Data as Infrastructure, Not an Afterthought

Schema markup — FAQPage, HowTo, Organization, and Article schema in particular — gives search and AI crawlers an explicit, machine-readable version of a page's content that reduces the chance of misinterpretation. It will not force a citation on its own, but it removes friction from the extraction process, which matters when a model is choosing between two similarly authoritative sources.

5. Track Citations, Not Just Rankings

Rank tracking tools were built for a world with one search results page. A modern B2B SEO consultant now needs a second, parallel tracking motion: running a client's top 15 to 20 buyer questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews on a monthly cadence and logging whether the client is cited, how it's described, and which competitors appear instead. This is manual today for most Indian consultants, but it is the closest available proxy for GEO performance until better tooling matures.

The consulting-practice implication: B2B marketing consultants in India who position themselves purely as "SEO consultants" are describing a shrinking slice of what clients actually need. Consultants who can speak fluently about GEO and AEO alongside SEO — and who can show a client their citation status inside ChatGPT and Perplexity, not just their Google ranking — are positioned as the more forward-looking, higher-value partner in every new-business conversation happening in India's B2B marketing services market right now.

Where to Start If You Manage Multiple B2B Clients

Do not attempt a full GEO and AEO overhaul across an entire client portfolio at once. Start with each client's 5 to 10 highest-intent, decision-stage pages — the ones a buyer reads right before a purchase conversation — and restructure those first for direct-answer clarity, named expertise, and schema. Run the citation-tracking exercise on those same pages monthly. This narrow, repeatable process produces visible, defensible results for a client review far faster than a site-wide rebuild, and it is the same 80/20 approach that made traditional SEO retainers sustainable for Indian consultants in the first place.
