What a Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) Consultant Actually Does in India

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  6 min read

"Generative Engine Optimisation consultant" is a job title that did not exist in most Indian marketing teams' vocabulary two years ago. It exists now because the underlying problem is real: a growing share of B2B research happens inside ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity conversations that never touch a search results page, and a brand that isn't built into how these models describe a category is quietly absent from deals it never knew it was competing for.

The Job Title Nobody Had Two Years Ago

GEO grew out of SEO the same way performance marketing grew out of print advertising: the underlying skill (understanding intent, structuring information for a system to retrieve and reuse) carried over, but the surface changed enough that a new specialisation formed around it. A GEO consultant in India today is usually someone with a strong SEO and content background who has additionally built a practice around how large language models select, summarise, and cite sources.

What a GEO Consultant Actually Delivers

Strip away the buzzwords and a competent GEO engagement produces a specific set of outputs:

  • A citation audit — running a client's top buyer questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews to see whether the brand appears, how it's described, and who is cited instead.
  • Content restructuring for the pages most likely to be pulled into AI answers: direct-answer openings, clear entity definitions, and specific data points models can quote confidently.
  • Structured data implementation — schema markup that makes a page's claims machine-readable rather than buried in prose.
  • Authorship and credibility signals — named experts, verifiable credentials, and specific numbers, because models weight source credibility heavily when choosing what to cite.
  • A recurring measurement cadence — because citation status changes month to month as models retrain and re-crawl, unlike a Google ranking that is comparatively stable.

How GEO Work Differs from a Traditional SEO Retainer

The research and content fundamentals overlap almost completely with good SEO. What differs is the measurement loop and the content format. A traditional SEO retainer optimises toward a rank-tracking dashboard with a monthly cadence. A GEO engagement optimises toward a citation-tracking exercise that currently has to be run manually, because no mainstream analytics tool yet reports "cited in ChatGPT" the way Google Search Console reports rankings. This is the main reason GEO consultants in India still charge as an addition to, not a replacement for, an SEO retainer.

What to Ask Before Hiring a GEO Consultant

  • Can they show a before/after citation audit for an existing client, not just a rankings report?
  • Do they understand your specific buyer's vocabulary well enough to write the exact questions a procurement head or CFO would ask an AI tool?
  • Will the engagement touch your existing content library, or does it only produce new content? (Refreshing existing high-authority pages is usually higher ROI than publishing net-new pages.)
  • Do they separate GEO reporting from SEO reporting, or fold everything into one vague "visibility" number that can't be acted on?

A useful filter: if a consultant's GEO pitch sounds identical to their SEO pitch with the word "AI" added, they likely haven't built the citation-tracking discipline that makes GEO a distinct, measurable practice rather than a rebrand.
