Content Strategy for Indian IT Services Companies to Win in AI Search

By Vikas Goyal  ·  August 2026  ·  7 min read

Enterprise buyers evaluating an Indian IT services or ITES vendor already do an unusually large amount of research before any sales conversation happens, often building an initial longlist of 8 to 12 vendors before a single call is scheduled. That longlist-building step is exactly where AI tools have inserted themselves fastest, which makes IT services one of the categories most exposed to — and with the most upside available from — a deliberate AI search content strategy.

Why This Category Is Different

Unlike a SaaS product with a self-serve trial, IT services engagements are evaluated on trust, domain depth, and delivery track record, none of which are easy to convey in a thin marketing page. This actually plays in favour of companies willing to publish specific, verifiable content, because generic "full-stack digital transformation partner" positioning (still common across Indian IT services websites) gives an AI model nothing distinctive to cite, while a case study with a named client outcome and a specific number does.

The Content That Actually Gets a Vendor Onto a Longlist

Named, Numbered Case Studies

"Reduced claims processing time by 40% for a mid-size US insurer over an 8-month engagement" is citable content. "We have delivered digital transformation for leading global enterprises" is not, because it contains no specific, checkable claim a model or a buyer can act on.

Capability-Specific Pages, Not One Generic Services Page

A buyer researching "SAP S/4HANA migration partner with BFSI experience in India" needs a page built around exactly that combination, not a broad services page that mentions SAP once in a bulleted list among thirty other capabilities.

Certifications and Partnerships as Structured Data

ISO certifications, cloud partner tiers (AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud), and industry-specific compliance credentials (HIPAA, GDPR readiness, RBI guidelines for BFSI clients) should be marked up as structured, verifiable facts, not buried in a paragraph, since these are exactly the kind of concrete, checkable signals that build model and buyer confidence simultaneously.

The Trap Most Indian IT Services Marketing Falls Into

A large share of Indian IT services websites use nearly identical language to describe themselves — "trusted technology partner," "end-to-end solutions," "customer-centric approach" — which is a content strategy failure independent of AI search, but AI search makes the cost of that failure sharper: a model summarising a category has no way to differentiate between vendors whose public content says the same thing, so it either omits the smaller player entirely or lists it without any distinguishing detail.

A Practical Starting Point

Pick the three capability areas that generate the most actual revenue today, not the three a company aspires to be known for, and build one deeply specific, evidence-backed page for each before broadening. This narrow, deep approach consistently outperforms a broad, shallow services page rebuild for both traditional SEO and AI citation purposes.

For agencies and consultants serving this sector: the single highest-leverage recommendation to give an Indian IT services client is almost always "replace generic capability claims with three specific, numbered case studies," because it is a content fix, not a technical or budget one, and it improves performance across SEO, AEO, and GEO simultaneously.
